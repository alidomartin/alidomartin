# The GCT vs flight time double standard

**Bridge:** The field rejects flight time as a CMJ jump-height proxy because in-air leg position contaminates it, yet accepts ground contact time in drop-jump RSI as if it were technique-clean. It is not.

**Potential hook:** If flight time is too dirty for jump height, drop-jump GCT should be too dirty for RSI. Pick a standard.

## Why this is non-obvious
RSI from drop jumps is treated as a **clean reactive-strength signal** in most S&C settings. But GCT is sensitive to drop height, foot-strike pattern, toe-touch contact mode, and instructional cue. These are exactly the kind of technique-driven artefacts the field cited when retiring flight time. McMahon's 2018 work fixed the vocabulary problem; nobody has fixed the methodological-double-standard problem. A short piece naming this could land hard with sport scientists who already feel the tension.
