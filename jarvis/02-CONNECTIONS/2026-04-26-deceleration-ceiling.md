# The deceleration ceiling on jump height

**Bridge:** Jump height is gated by **braking peak**, not by propulsion. An athlete who cannot decelerate in unweighting cannot load the propulsive phase, so adding more concentric work has nowhere to land.

**Potential hook:** Most coaches treat low CMJ jump height as a propulsion problem. The force trace usually says it is a braking problem.

## Why this is non-obvious
The default S&C reflex for a flat jump trace is "more triple extension" — Cleans, Trap Bar Jumps, Pin Squats. That works when propulsion is the limiter. When the limiter is **eccentric tolerance**, those same exercises produce no transfer because the athlete still cannot reach the braking peak (~1.75×BW on a clean CMJ) that pre-loads the propulsive phase. The fix is eccentric overload (drops, accentuated eccentrics, depth-controlled tempo work), not more concentric volume. The force trace tells you which it is in 30 seconds; most coaches never look.
