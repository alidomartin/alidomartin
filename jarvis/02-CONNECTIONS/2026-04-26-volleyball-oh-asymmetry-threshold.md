# Volleyball OH asymmetry needs a sport-specific threshold

**Bridge:** The 10% asymmetry rule of thumb was derived from symmetric-sport populations. Volleyball outside hitters are asymmetric by sport demand — one-foot block jumps, contralateral attack landings — so applying the generic threshold mislabels healthy adaptation as risk.

**Potential hook:** Your OH's "asymmetry" might be the sport, not the injury risk.

## Why this is non-obvious
Most clinics and S&C departments apply the **10% asymmetry threshold** uniformly across sports and positions. For volleyball OHs the loading pattern is structurally asymmetric, so a 10–20% landing-impulse difference may be adaptation, not deficit. The opposite is also true: a "passing" 8% on a player whose sport demands the asymmetry could mean they have *lost* the trained pattern and are at higher risk than the number suggests.
