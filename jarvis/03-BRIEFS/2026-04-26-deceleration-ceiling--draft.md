# Most coaches treat low jump height as a propulsion problem. The force trace usually says it is a braking problem.

You add **Cleans**. You add **Trap Bar Jumps**. You add **Pin Squats**. The athlete's CMJ jump height does not move.

The reflex is to add more.

Look at the force trace before you do.

## The mechanical fact most programming ignores

A clean **CMJ** peaks at roughly **1.75×BW braking force** before it reaches **2.1×BW propulsive force**. The braking peak does not just precede propulsion in time. It pre-loads it. The eccentric phase stores elastic energy and recruits the muscle spindles that fire the concentric phase.

If the **braking peak** is not there, the propulsive peak has nothing to launch from.

Loaded triple extension cannot save what triple flexion never loaded.

## The 30-second diagnostic

Pull up the athlete's last clean CMJ trace. Run this:

1. Is the propulsive peak under **2.0×BW**? Yes, the jump is flat.
2. Is the braking peak under **1.5×BW**? If yes, the limiter is eccentric. Stop here.
3. If braking is fine and propulsion is flat, then the limiter is concentric. Now Cleans, Trap Bar Jumps, and Pin Squats earn their place.

Most flat traces fail at step two, not step three.

## What to prescribe when the limiter is eccentric

Eccentric tolerance does not respond to more concentric volume. It responds to **eccentric overload**:

- **Depth drops** at progressively higher heights, controlled landings.
- **Accentuated eccentric loading** — heavier on the way down than on the way up (release straps on a Smith bar, partner-assisted concentric).
- **Tempo work** with a 3–5 second eccentric on squats, RDLs, and split squats.
- **Tier the eccentric stimulus** before adding any new triple-extension volume.

Re-test the CMJ in 4 weeks. The braking peak should move first. Jump height follows.

## Why this matters

The default S&C reflex for a flat jump is "more triple extension." That works when propulsion is the limiter. When the limiter is **eccentric tolerance**, the same exercises produce no transfer and you spend a block of programming wondering why an athlete is not getting more athletic.

The force trace tells you which it is. In 30 seconds.
