# Skill: Generate Brief

## Trigger phrases
"generate a brief for [topic]" / "brief this connection" / "make a brief"

## Process
Create a content brief with exactly these five fields. Save to `03-BRIEFS/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` with tag `#ready-to-write`.

### ONE THING
The single insight this piece is built around.
Must be one sentence. If it cannot be expressed in one sentence, the idea is not ready.
**Push back if the One Thing is fuzzy.** Do not write a brief from a fuzzy premise.

### PROOF
The most specific real example, number, or result that proves the One Thing.
Vague proof invalidates the brief. Real numbers only.
For Alido: prefer force-plate metrics, group means, individual athlete deltas, or peer-reviewed findings with citation.

### READER TRANSFORMATION
What does the reader (an S&C coach, sport scientist, or serious athlete) know or feel at the end that they did not before?
If this cannot be stated clearly, the piece has no reason to exist.

### THREE HOOKS (ranked)
Different in approach and tone. Rank by how hard they stop the scroll.
- **Hook 1 — aggressive:** a contrarian or pointed claim.
- **Hook 2 — curious:** a clean question or a number that demands explanation.
- **Hook 3 — personal:** something Alido saw in the lab or with an athlete this week.

### THREE CLOSERS (ranked)
Rank by urgency and memorability.
**Write the closer before the middle. Always.**
