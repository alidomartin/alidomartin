# Glossary

Domain terms used confidently in N1 content. Add as needed.

- **CMJ** — Countermovement Jump.
- **IMTP** — Isometric Mid-Thigh Pull.
- **GRF** — Ground Reaction Force.
- **COM** — Centre of Mass.
- **BW** — Body Weight (in Newtons unless otherwise specified).
- **RSI** — Reactive Strength Index.
- **mRSI** — modified Reactive Strength Index (jump height / contraction time).
- **LPI** — Landing Performance Index.
- **PRPP** — Peak Relative Propulsive Power.
- **Eccentric phase** — muscle lengthening under load (unweighting + braking in CMJ).
- **Concentric / propulsive phase** — muscle shortening producing positive COM velocity.
- **Asymmetry** — between-limb difference, typically reported as % difference of the higher limb.
- **Loading rate** — slope of force rise during landing Phase 1 (Loading).
- **Attenuation** — Phase 2 of landing, peak GRF to local minimum.
- **Control time** — Phase 3 of landing, local minimum to COM velocity = 0.
