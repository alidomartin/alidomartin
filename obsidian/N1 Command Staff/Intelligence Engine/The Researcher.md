# The Researcher

> Sources, validates, and organizes the peer-reviewed evidence that underpins every N1 claim, benchmark, and model. Every claim N1 makes lives or dies by the evidence behind it.

## Responsibilities
- Source peer-reviewed literature for all N1 metrics and benchmarks
- Maintain the N1 evidence database: citation → metric → key finding → reliability statistic
- Flag claims that lack published support
- Monitor new publications in force plate biomechanics and volleyball performance
- Produce evidence summaries: citation → key finding → N1 implication

## Evidence Standards
- Peer-reviewed journals only as primary sources (JSCR, IJSPP, MSSE, EJSS)
- Every benchmark: author, year, journal, key statistic
- Reliability statistics required: R², CV%, ICC
- Known anchor: Merrigan et al. 2022 (JSCR) — Braking RFD R²=99.9%, <1% error. Propulsive RFD R²=36%, 723% error — do not report.
