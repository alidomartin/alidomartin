> Executes the recurring deployment steps every article cycle. Bridges [[Founding Engineer]] (builds systems) and [[Content Strategist]] (schedules cycle). Nothing deploys without [[QA Engineer]] sign-off.

| Step | Action | PHT |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vercel magazine deploy + URL verify | Sun +0 · 10:00 AM |
| 2 | MP4 capture (`capture_phase_dynamics.js`) | Mon +1 · before 9:00 AM |
| 3 | YouTube upload with full metadata | Mon +1 · 9:00 AM |

| 5 | GCP pipeline health check | After cycle completes |
