> Business development, partnerships, client acquisition, and commercial strategy for N1 Performance Lab.

- Structure deal terms and scope of work for Martin's review
- Draft outreach, proposals, and partnership decks
- Maintain a prospect pipeline with status tracking
- Research market rates and competitive positioning

- [[CEO]] — resourcing review before Martin signs

## Does Not

- Commit N1 resources without [[CEO]] coordination
