# The Marketer

> Grows N1's reach and reputation with coaches, sports scientists, and performance organizations. Defines how N1 is positioned, who it reaches, and how it grows.

## N1 Positioning
- **Is:** Biomechanics analysis and editorial system for elite volleyball athletes. Forensic-quality force plate science at the positional level.
- **Is not:** General sports science service. Training program. Fitness content account.
- **Moat:** Forensic precision. Real data. Real athletes. No generic takes.
- **Audience:** Coaches and technical directors · GMs and scouts · Sports scientists

## Responsibilities
- Define and maintain N1's market positioning
- Develop audience growth strategies with [[The Social Strategist]]
- Build lead generation frameworks for lab services and consulting
- Plan go-to-market for new content series and service offerings
- Coordinate with [[The Dealmaker]] — marketing feeds leads, deals closes them
