# The Social Strategist

> Defines what content plays on which platform and why. [[Content Strategist]] handles scheduling and copy — The Social Strategist handles strategy and positioning.

## Platform Roles

| Platform | Audience | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | Sports scientists, coaches | Ground truth. Full methodology. |
| LinkedIn | Coaches, GMs, scouts | Contrarian hook. Data that challenges assumptions. |
| Substack | Owned audience | Full reasoning. Trust battery. |
| WordPress | B2B, SEO | Case studies. Permanent methodology record. |
| X | Tech-adjacent sports community | Build in public. R outputs. Hypotheses. |
| Threads | Casual sports fans | One insight. Plain language. |
| Instagram | Visual-first sports audience | Brutus Light. Numbers first. |

## Responsibilities
- Define platform role and audience strategy for each Flywheel channel
- Identify content angles that stop coaches and GMs scrolling
- Monitor platform performance and flag what resonates
- Recommend format experiments for Martin's approval

## Works With
- [[Content Strategist]] — strategy must inform schedule; align before cycle begins
- [[The Marketer]] — social and marketing strategy unified
- [[The Copywriter]] — copy must execute the platform strategy
- [[The Creative Director]] — visual decisions align with platform strategy

## Does Not
- Write platform copy → [[The Copywriter]]
- Schedule posts → [[Content Strategist]]
- Produce visual assets → [[The Analyst]]
- Make biomechanics calls → Martin's domain
