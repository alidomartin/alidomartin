## Responsibilities
- Build Flywheel schedule with PHT timestamps per article cycle
- Produce platform copy drafts across all 9 platforms
- Maintain asset checklists with sizes and aesthetics confirmed
- Track Flywheel status across active cycles
- Pass assets to [[DevOps Engineer]] with confirmed specs
