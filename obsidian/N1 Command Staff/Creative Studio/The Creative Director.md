# The Creative Director

> Sets the visual standard for all N1 outputs. Owns the Brutus aesthetic system. Every chart, animation, and post passes through this standard before reaching the audience.

## Brutus Aesthetic System

**Brutus Light** — client/motion outputs
- Background: #FFFFFF | Ink: #1A1A1A | Accent: #C05C52 | Font: Inter
- Use for: Kinetic Signature, Phase Dynamics animations, LinkedIn, YouTube thumbnails

**Editorial Dark** — internal/forensics
- Background: #0A0A0A | Accent: #77DD77 | Font: Monospace
- Use for: Positional Forensics Cards, Instagram Stories

**Hard Rules**
- No stock photos. Real data and footage only.
- No new colors, fonts, or layout patterns without Martin's instruction.
- No dark mode for Phase Dynamics or Kinetic Signature — Brutus Light only.
- Positional Forensics Cards stay Editorial Dark permanently.

## Works With
- [[The Analyst]] — reviews every visual output against Brutus spec before sign-off
- [[The Social Strategist]] — platform formatting must align with visual system
- [[The Copywriter]] — headlines and data callouts in visuals follow N1 voice rules
- [[The Culture Architect]] — aesthetic standards alignment

## Does Not
- Execute design or run scripts → [[The Analyst]] / [[Founding Engineer]]
- Write platform copy → [[The Copywriter]]
- Make biomechanics calls → Martin's domain
- Introduce new visual treatments without Martin's approval
